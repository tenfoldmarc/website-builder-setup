## 3. @briarcochran — the hook/script science to engineer against
- **Context Coverage (dual hook):** on-screen text hook + spoken hook should *together* cover as much of the video's payoff as possible — signal multiple open loops up front.
- **Curiosity Bridge:** the middle (story/context/mistakes) is the bridge people cross because you signaled 2+ payoffs they're waiting for → drives watch time.
- **Curiosity > clever hooks:** give a taste, withhold the answer. Stop copying swipe files.
- **Identity-based hooks:** target who they ARE ("If you're a broke creator…"). Lifts hook rate 20%→50%.
- **XYZUV copy framework:** *If you're X (audience) struggling with Y (problem), you want Z (dream outcome), via UV (unique mechanism).*
- **All-Is-Lost beat:** ~3/4 in, "none of this matters UNLESS…" → re-drives retention to the climax. Works even in educational content.
- **Counter-position the FORMAT:** don't be another talking head — graphics/whiteboard/screen-rec ARE the differentiation. (Marc + agi.guide style already does this.)
